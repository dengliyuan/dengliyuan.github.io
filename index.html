Biography

Liyuan Deng (邓立原) is currently a Ph.D. student with the Department of Control Science and Engineering, College of Electronic and Information Engineering, Tongji University, Shanghai, China, and also a Dual Ph.D. student with the Department of Computing, Faculty of Computer and Mathematical Sciences, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong, China. His research interests include edge computing, mobile crowdsensing, multi-agent systems, and deep reinforcement learning.
