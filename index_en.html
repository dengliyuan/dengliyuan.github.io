Biography

Liyuan Deng received the B.S. degree in Automation and the M.S. degree in Control Science and Engineering (supervised by Prof. Li Li) from Tongji University, Shanghai, China, in 2021 and 2024, respectively. He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Control Science and Engineering at the Department of Control Science and Engineering, Tongji University supervised by Prof. Jie Chen, and the Dual Ph.D. degree at the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University supervised by Prof. Jiannong Cao. His research interests include multi-agent systems, mobile computing, and deep reinforcement learning.
